You've narrowed it down to two family organizer apps, and both promise to get the whole house on one calendar. Cozi has been around forever. FamilyWall does a little of everything. You just want to know which one to download before the next permission slip disappears.

Here's the short answer on Cozi vs FamilyWall: pick Cozi if your family runs on lists and meal plans. Pick FamilyWall if you want a family hub with chat, photos and location next to the calendar. And if the real problem is that your dates are buried in emails, neither one fixes that for free.

I'm a dad of two. Every fact below comes from Cozi's and FamilyWall's own websites, help pages and App Store listings, as of October 2026. Prices change, so check before you buy.

## Price: Cozi vs FamilyWall

Both have a free version. Cozi's free version is supported by ads and sponsors, and its App Store listing says Gold unlocks events more than 30 days ahead. Gold is $39 a year on Cozi's plan page. Cozi also sells a Max plan with AI features; its own pages list two slightly different Max prices, so check the current one.

FamilyWall says most of its features are free. Premium is $4.99 a month or $44.99 a year on FamilyWall's website, with a 30-day free trial. The App Store lists the monthly plan at a different price, so compare before you subscribe.

**Winner:** close. Cozi Gold costs less per year; FamilyWall gives you a longer trial.

## Calendar and Google Calendar sync

Both apps color-code the calendar by person. Cozi gives each family member a color dot, and FamilyWall lets you view one person's schedule or the whole family at once.

Sync is where they split. Cozi offers read-only integration with Google, Outlook and Apple Calendar. You can share other calendars into Cozi, and Cozi into them, but each direction is its own read-only feed. Cozi says incoming updates arrive within about an hour, and Google can take up to 48 hours to pick up changes from Cozi.

FamilyWall lists Google and Outlook Calendar sync as a Premium feature. For Apple, it uses a shared iCloud link, and FamilyWall says changes on your iOS calendar show up in FamilyWall.

**Winner:** FamilyWall, if you pay for Premium and live in Google or Outlook.

## Getting events from email

This is the feature that matters most if your inbox is where the schedule starts.

FamilyWall Premium has Magic Import. Each family Circle gets one unique email address. Forward an email to it, from the address linked to your profile, and FamilyWall pulls out the title, date, time, location and notes. It also takes PDFs and images.

Cozi's Max plan lists AI Event Import, which reads invitations and calendar details from your inbox and adds them to your Cozi calendar.

**Winner:** both now offer it on paid plans. FamilyWall's version works on emails you forward to your family's FamilyWall address ([FamilyWall help](https://support.familywall.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001301286-add-events-to-your-calendar-by-forwarding-an-email)).

## Everything else

- **Cozi:** shared grocery and to-do lists, a recipe library, a meal planner and agenda emails. Gold adds month view, search and change notifications. Cozi doesn't support photo or file attachments.
- **FamilyWall:** shopping and to-do lists, messaging, a family gallery, contacts, recipes and a family locator. Premium adds a meal planner, budget tracker, documents, real-time location and 25GB of storage.

## Which should you choose?

- **Choose Cozi** if your family's life runs on lists, recipes and meal plans, you have a big family, and you don't mind ads or read-only sync.
- **Choose FamilyWall** if you want messaging, photos and location in the same app, and Google or Outlook sync is worth paying for.
