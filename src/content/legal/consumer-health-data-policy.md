# Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 9, 2026

This policy covers "consumer health data" as Washington's My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373) and similar state laws (including Nevada and Connecticut) define it. It adds to our [Privacy Policy](https://cleanmailapp.com/privacy), which covers everything else. Calibrate LLC ("we") operates Cleanmail.

## 1. What health data we collect
Cleanmail does not ask for health information. Some can still reach us inside the school and activity email you choose to include in your digest, for example a nurse note, an allergy notice, a doctor's or therapist's appointment, or a learning-support meeting. If you add a health provider as a sender, the app tells you before you choose it that its emails may include health details, and choosing it and saving is your consent. Health appointments you type in yourself are collected too.

## 2. Why we collect it and how we use it
Only to provide what you asked for: your digest, your calendar events and your reminders. We never use it for advertising, never sell it, and never use it to build a profile of you or your child.

## 3. Where it comes from
From the Gmail messages of the senders you pick, from items you add yourself (typing, voice, photo or PDF), and from household members who share your digest.

## 4. Who we share it with
We do not share consumer health data with third parties. Our service providers process it only on our instructions to run Cleanmail: Anthropic (AI summaries, no training on your data), Supabase (storage) and Google (Calendar sync you turn on). The people in your household see your shared digest. We disclose data when the law requires it (Privacy Policy, Section 11).

## 5. Your rights
You can ask to confirm whether we hold your consumer health data, see it, get a list of who it was shared with, have it deleted, or withdraw consent. Most of it you can delete yourself in the app, or by deleting your account. Otherwise email support@cleanmailapp.com from your account's address. We respond within 45 days, and tell you if we need up to 45 more. If we decline, reply with "Appeal" in the subject and we respond within 45 days. If your appeal is denied, you can contact the Washington Attorney General at atg.wa.gov.
